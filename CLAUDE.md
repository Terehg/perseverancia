# Perseverancia

3D simulation of NASA's Perseverance rover exploring the surface of Mars.
Simulación en 3D del rover Perseverance de la NASA explorando la superficie de Marte.

## Project goals
- Render a 3D Martian terrain.
- Model the Perseverance rover (body, wheels, arm, mast camera).
- Let the user drive the rover and explore the surface.
